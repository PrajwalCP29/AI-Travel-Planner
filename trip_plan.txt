 5-Day Goa Trip from Mumbai (Budget: ₹20,000)

This itinerary focuses on a balance of relaxation, exploration, and enjoying Goa's vibrant culture, while staying within a ₹20,000 budget for a solo traveller.  Adjustments can be made for couples or groups, impacting the overall cost.

Transportation (₹4,000 - ₹6,000):

 Mumbai to Goa: Overnight sleeper bus (₹1,000 - ₹1,500 each way).  Consider options like Paulo Travels or Neeta Tours for relatively comfortable journeys.
 Local Transport in Goa:  Rent a scooter (₹300-₹500 per day) for maximum flexibility and exploring hidden gems.  Negotiate prices.  Alternatively, use local buses or taxis for specific journeys, but this can be less convenient.

Accommodation (₹5,000 - ₹7,000):

 North Goa (Calangute/Baga/Anjuna):  Plenty of budget-friendly guesthouses, hostels, and homestays available. Expect to pay ₹800-₹1,500 per night for a decent room.  Pre-book during peak season. Consider Zostel (hostel) or Airbnb for budget options.
 South Goa (Palolem/Agonda): Slightly pricier than North Goa, but offers a more tranquil experience. Look for beach huts or homestays.

Food (₹4,000 - ₹5,000):

 Local Eateries: Embrace the delicious and affordable Goan cuisine at local restaurants and beach shacks.  Try seafood thalis, fish curry rice, and local snacks. Budget ₹500-₹800 per day.
 Street Food: Explore street food options like Pav Bhaji, Ros Omelette, and Chorizo Pao for cheap and tasty treats.
 Supermarkets: Purchase water, snacks, and fruits from supermarkets to save money.


Itinerary:

Day 1: Arrival & North Goa Exploration

 Arrive in Goa (Panjim or Mapusa bus stand) in the morning.
 Travel to your chosen accommodation in North Goa (e.g., Calangute).
 Check in, freshen up, and head to the beach for relaxation and swimming.
 Enjoy the sunset at Calangute beach and have dinner at a beach shack.

Day 2: North Goa Beaches & Fort Aguada

 Rent a scooter.
 Explore Baga and Anjuna beaches, known for their vibrant atmosphere and flea markets.
 Visit Fort Aguada for historical insights and panoramic views.
 Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant in Anjuna.
 Evening: Explore the Saturday Night Market at Arpora (seasonal).

Day 3: Spice Plantation & Dudhsagar Falls

 Day trip to a spice plantation (e.g., Sahakari Spice Farm). Learn about spices and enjoy a traditional Goan meal.
 Visit Dudhsagar Falls (consider a jeep safari tour - ₹500-₹800, including entry fees).
 Return to North Goa in the evening.

Day 4: South Goa Exploration

 Ride down to South Goa.
 Visit Palolem beach – relax, swim, and enjoy the serene atmosphere.
 Explore Patnem beach or Agonda beach for quieter alternatives.
 Enjoy a delicious seafood dinner at a beach shack in Palolem.

Day 5: Departure

 Enjoy a final breakfast in Goa.
 Depending on your bus schedule, visit a local market for souvenirs.
 Travel back to Mumbai by overnight bus.


Tips for Staying within Budget:

 Travel during the off-season: Avoid peak season (October-February) for better deals on accommodation and transport.
 Negotiate prices:  Bargaining is common, especially for scooter rentals and souvenirs.
 Eat at local restaurants:  Avoid fancy restaurants and stick to local eateries for affordable and authentic Goan cuisine.
 Limit alcohol consumption:  Alcohol can significantly add to your expenses.
 Carry sufficient cash: ATMs might not be readily available in all areas.
 Pack light: This will save you money on baggage fees if you're flying.


Contingency (₹1,000):  Keep a buffer for unexpected expenses or unforeseen circumstances.

This itinerary provides a framework. Customize it based on your preferences and interests. Enjoy your trip to Goa!
